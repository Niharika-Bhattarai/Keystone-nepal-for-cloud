## Unresolved research that blocks numeric rules

1. Exact pilot municipality/ward/zone and operative consolidated local standards.
2. Coverage/FAR, setbacks, height, partial-floor and parking accounting for that parcel.
3. Actual opening/daylight/ventilation, stair and separation provisions and exceptions.
4. Adopted seismic edition and allowable structural route for the selected storeys, infill and site conditions.
5. Utility/service and permit-document requirements for this residential project.
6. Current applicable Kathmandu/Kaski price schedules and comparable local quotes. No district unit prices were invented or imported from another district.
7. Owner's Vaastu source and interpretation. No unverified web doctrine has been encoded as a rule.

Government listings are a starting point. If an online PDF is unavailable, request the full document from the owner/local design team. An AI summary or another project's approval is not a substitute for the applicable rule text and professional interpretation.
