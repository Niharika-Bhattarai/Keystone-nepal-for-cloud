All ten geometries differ but still share a rectangular full-floor massing family. **No option passes all architectural, structural, legal and service checks.** The listed column count is a *planning reservation*, not a proven economical or seismic frame. The maximum dedicated stair bay corner-axis span is **4,235 mm**, below the owner's **4,267 mm (14 ft)** preference; there are short grid spans elsewhere. Plan 5 needs 16, not the owner's preferred 9–12, because the denser upper program currently occupies the full 9.25 m depth. An engineer must design actual columns, beams, slab openings, tank/foundation separation and seismic system under NBC 105:2025.

The WC proportions were checked against the owner's 4–5 × 7–9 ft convention and the supplied examples: `Reference Drawings/2.3.2021_Jivendra Residence Design.pdf` page 2 shows about 5 × 9 ft and 4 ft 9 in × 9 ft W/Cs; `COMPLILED PDF 2.5.2020.pdf` page 2 shows about 8 ft 1 in × 4 ft 6 in; `FEB 3.pdf` page 1 shows about 5 × 9 ft and 4 ft 9 in × 9 ft. Those are precedents, not a municipal minimum. A 750 mm bathroom opening is retained from the supplied NBC 206:2024 section 2.2.1 A reference rather than the owner's approximate 700 mm. Fixture clearances and door swings remain untested.

The tank calculation is `2.100 × 3.900 × 0.977 = 8.00163 m³ = 8,001.63 L` net, with a provisional 200 mm freeboard. It fits *inside the dedicated stair rectangle* in plan, not on a separate landing-beam projection. Tank access, excavation, loading and footing interaction need engineering. The stair uses 16 risers over 3 m: 187.5 mm rise, two 8-riser flights, 7 × 255 mm plan treads per flight, 1,000 mm intermediate landing and 1,400 mm floor-arrival pad. Headroom, beam clearance, egress and landing/door swings remain open.

The next high-value topology work is a dense rental ground-floor parking/entry configuration that stays within verified coverage and keeps all rental flats independent, then owner-floor attached bath and balcony. After that, model flush-neighbor edges and the adopted municipality/road/height setback rule together so every opening is either permitted on the actual surveyed parcel or removed. Resolve the partial top as enclosed rooms versus roof terrace, produce fixture/furniture and kitchen counter plans, and coordinate structural, MEP, elevations, sections and CAD.
