## Geometry and drawing sequence

1. Resolve the **four-column-room** and direct living-entry geometry using `roomPlanner.js`, `frameGrid.js`, `candidateSearch.js`, and `validateNepalPlan.js`. Do not optimize furnishings to mask a bad frame or room arrangement.
2. Add balcony geometry as a separate slab/void/exposure category in `siteEnvelope.js` and `areaLedger.js`; connect balcony doors through `spatialReservations.js` and model them in `wallGeometry.js`. Treat projection/setback and FAR applicability as unresolved until the parcel's adopted rule is reviewed.
3. Create fixture geometry for kitchen, bathroom and living in a dedicated interior-placement module. Use `room.clearBox`, door/window intervals and column boxes; test non-overlap, circulation and fixture-specific clearances. Annotate `reviewSheet.js` with actual placed fixtures only.
4. Add actual wall finish layers and floor build-ups to the geometry model. Recompute finished dimensions and quantity takeoffs from the same geometry; keep unselected material prices and reinforcement out of the estimate.
5. Coordinate architecture, RC, MEP reservations, elevations/section and 3D from one revisioned plan. Permit drawings follow local authority and professional review; the present review sheet is a spatial hypothesis.

The reproducible owner/rental fixtures and checks are in `local/backend-keystone/test/nepal-residential-construction.test.js`; the local output is `local/runtime/nepal-plan-review/index.html` and `summary.json`.
