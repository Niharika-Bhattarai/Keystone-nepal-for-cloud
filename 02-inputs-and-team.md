# Owner inputs, local team and decisions

The 30-day clock starts when the minimum package below and the reviewing professionals are available. Missing inputs change the deliverable or schedule; do not compensate with invented values.

## Minimum package to start

1. Confirm KMC or name the first permit municipality, ward and actual pilot parcel.
2. Provide a measured site survey, true-north reference, road information, levels and neighboring conditions. An area number alone is insufficient.
3. Appoint the Nepal architect/design lead and structural engineer responsible for the real project. Confirm their registration and local submission eligibility directly; the [Nepal Engineering Council](https://www.nec.gov.np/) provides a registration-status lookup.
4. Provide one recent approved complete drawing package from that municipality and its submission checklist, with permission to use it as a reference.
5. Confirm owner program, number/use of levels, parking, budget and intended future expansion.
6. Supply the Vaastu reference or introduce the consultant who will resolve interpretations.

## Detailed request list

| Priority / due | What to obtain | Required detail / format | Why / owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| P0, before Day 1 | Actual pilot brief | Family needs, rooms per floor, separate units, accessibility, parking, future levels, spending range | Owner and architect define the design problem |
| P0, before Day 1 | Survey and parcel records | Licensed/measured boundary CAD or coordinates, units, north, levels, area reconciliation, road/ROW, access, easements, adjoining walls/windows/heights | Surveyor; prevents designing an impossible envelope |
| P0, Days 1–3 | Current local standards | Complete municipality standards, all amendments, planning zone/road provisions, written clarification of coverage/FAR/parking/boundary cases | Architect/local authority; establish applicable rules |
| P0, Days 1–3 | Permit procedure | Current checklist, application forms, sheets/scales/file formats, who signs, local designer listing requirements, fees, required reports | Architect; defines actual submission deliverable |
| P0, Days 1–3 | National code PDFs | Current adopted NBC 105, 206 and relevant RC, material, load, fire, sanitary/electrical provisions; adoption confirmation | Structural engineer and architect; public links are collected separately |
| P0, schedule on Day 1 | Ground/site investigation | Engineer-specified soil/geotechnical evidence and hazard assessment, groundwater and foundation recommendations as required | Structural engineer; cannot infer soil from city name |
| P0, Day 3 | Vaastu profile | Book title/edition and authorized pages, or consultant's written rules; identify nonnegotiable versus advisory preferences | Owner/consultant; implement consistent directional checks |
| P0, Day 3 | Approved reference project | Native DWG with xrefs/fonts/plot styles, exported PDF, structural calculations and details, MEP where available, approval/redline history | Architect/engineer; exposes real drawing and review expectations |
| P1, Day 5 | Broader sample set | Target 8–12 complete homes across both locations; prioritize 3/4/5/6 aana, narrow plots, 2.5/3/3.5 storeys and different parking | Architect; benchmark corpus, not a training set without permission |
| P1, Day 5 | Construction assemblies | Brick/block options, wall/plaster/finish thickness, roof build-up, waterproofing, local windows/doors, tank and railing details | Architect/contractor; model actual construction |
| P1, Day 5 | Service information | Water source/pressure/availability, sewer or approved onsite disposal, road drain/outfall, power service, LPG/cooking, hot water/solar/AC | Owner and MEP reviewers |
| P1, Day 10 | Independent BOQ | Measured quantities tied to drawings; reinforcement schedule; deduction/waste conventions | QS/estimator; meaningful quantity-error reference |
| P1, Day 10 | Price evidence | Current Kathmandu/Kaski district schedules if applicable, 2–3 supplier/contractor quotes for key items, date/tax/freight/labor scope | QS/owner; NPR estimate calibration |
| P1, Day 10 | Local aesthetics | 10–20 permitted-use exterior/interior references; preferred palettes and disliked examples | Owner/architect; avoid a generic US facade recolor |
| P2, before pilot release | Product operations | Nepal contact details, language review, support process, commercial offer, privacy/retention and sharing permissions | Owner; avoid leaking owner/property documents in examples |

If only PDFs exist, begin with them, but native CAD accelerates measurement and reduces ambiguity. A PDF can hide units, scales, missing xrefs and undocumented revisions. Remove personal identifiers from general benchmark copies; retain controlled originals for the actual authorized submission workflow.

## Team and capacity assumption

This is a staffing estimate, not a quoted cost. Around **70–100 person-days** distributed across people can fit a month; it cannot fit one engineer's month.

| Role | Indicative person-days | Responsibility |
|---|---:|---|
| Lead backend/geometry engineer | 18–22 | Site constraints, multi-level generation, common model, integration |
| CAD/geometry engineer | 12–18 | Stairs/sections, drawing package, model coordination |
| Frontend/full-stack engineer | 10–15 | Nepal survey, preview, issue resolution and locale |
| QA/data engineer | 8–12 | Fixtures, audits, quantity references, regression evidence |
| Nepal architect / submission lead | 8–12 | Municipality interpretation, design review, drawing issue and submission |
| Nepal structural engineer | 7–12 | Site assessment, analysis, RC/foundation/reinforcement design and review |
| MEP reviewer(s) | 4–7 | Pilot systems design and calculations, coordination |
| QS/estimator | 2–4 | Measurement conventions, BOQ and price audit |

Some roles can be combined, but independent professional checking must remain meaningful. Confirm reviewer availability, fieldwork/lab lead times and holidays before assigning dates.

## Decisions to settle by Day 3

- Exact submission municipality and parcel; whether a municipal pre-submission discussion is available.
- Pilot occupancy and levels: do not defer rental-unit classification or future-floor loads.
- Which Vaastu reference controls; how to resolve conflicting essential preferences.
- Required engineered analysis, geotechnical inputs and drawing list for this project.
- Who signs architectural, structural and services documents and accepts revisions.
- Whether DWG is required; DXF export is not native DWG. Use an authorized CAD conversion/edit workflow and test it.
- Minimum supported site/brief domain; unsupported cases get an early explanation and a review path.

## What can wait

Commercial buildings, basements, arbitrary steep sites, all-municipality support, automated reinforcement design, national price coverage, full US MEP release and new local payment integration should not consume the permit pilot's critical path.

If the budget supports only one software developer and occasional review, treat the month as a narrower architectural prototype plus a largely professionally prepared pilot submission set. Reschedule broad generation automation explicitly; do not describe that smaller delivery as universal coverage.
