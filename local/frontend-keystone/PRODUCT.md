# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: homeowners and first-time home builders who know what they want in a house but cannot draw it. They arrive before hiring anyone, usually at home on a laptop or phone, often with a partner or family, trying to turn a vague idea into something concrete to react to and discuss.

Secondary (as recipients, not buyers): the designers, builders and architects these homeowners take the drawings to. They judge whether the output is credible.

## Product Purpose

Keystone AI turns a plain-language brief into a first working house concept: a dimensioned floor plan for every level, all four elevations, an exterior render, a CAD-ready DXF and a concept cost range, often in under a minute. Success is a visitor who leaves with drawings they understand and can take into a real design conversation.

## Positioning

The plans are generated by a deterministic engine, not an image model: it builds a room program, tests footprints, lays out zones, places stairs and openings, and only shows layouts that pass connectivity and room-count checks. Every output (plan, elevations, estimate, render) comes from the same geometry, with agreement checked explicitly during conversion; renderer parity is not yet complete.

## Operating Context

- Flow: guided survey (5 steps) or plain sentence → generated plan with a layout score and alternatives → elevations → optional exterior render, plain-language refinement, DXF and cost estimate.
- Anonymous: view floor plans. Free account: saved projects and watermarked quick 3D. Pro: elevations, renders, refinements, estimate and downloads.
- Agreed launch pricing: Pro $49/month, including three photoreal houses per month. Real billing and paid bakes remain disabled in this isolated conversion until their accounting and ownership work is verified.
- The studio lives inside the marketing site as a full-screen overlay.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Stack: Vite 8 + React 19 SPA (`frontend/`), Express API on Cloud Run (`backend/`), frontend on Vercel. Conversion happens in `frontend-keystone/` against `backend-keystone/`. The existing frontend/main and backend/master remain unchanged; no merges or cutover are authorized.
- Outputs are concept aids, not construction documents: not permit-ready, stamped or engineered.
- The engine supports a defined set of house families; some briefs return no valid layout. Copy must not promise every house.
- Quick 3D comes from the backend plan model. Geometry parity (including saved furniture rotation) is still being audited. Photoreal Blender jobs exist as a prototype; durable owned jobs, credits and Downloads are not complete. The previous final bake took about 26 minutes on its test laptop, not a measured cloud service SLA.
- Every engine SVG must go through `safeSvg()` (sanitize + id scoping) before injection. CSP forbids inline scripts.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: **Keystone AI** (the keystone is the stone that locks an arch; the name ChatGPT and Bing already send visitors to). The name and its files (site, exports, workbook, drawings) all say Keystone AI; the Parti Studio name is shelved (`docs/shelf/parti-studio/` in the repository root).
- Mark: a round arch of voussoirs on coursed piers, locked by a brass keystone; it animates (set, dropped in, one glint) and doubles as the working indicator. Geometry and files: `brand/README.md`.
- Visual direction chosen by the user: slightly frosted liquid glass over the house imagery, from the approved prototype.
- Voice: plain, specific, calm. Honest about limits and about what is gated.

## Evidence on Hand

- 11 real engine plans with elevations baked by `scripts/build-demo-plans.js` (`public/demo/`).
- Real engine output for the 3 bed / 2 story / 2,400 sq ft brief: score 100, two alternatives at 99, estimate $360,171–$508,473 (2026-09-28, after the foundation-quantity fix; the earlier $462,500–$652,937 priced foundations at 4x). A photoreal cutaway model of the same plan (roof off, upper floor lifted) is on the home hero.
- One sample exterior render (`public/images/keystone_study_render.png`); it is brick while the engine's elevations for the demo brief are farmhouse, so they do not match.
- Engine facts: ~36k lines across 153 modules, 552 automated tests, 12 plan validators, rules drawn from five architecture reference books.
- Absent: customer testimonials, user counts, press, case studies. Do not invent them.

## Product Principles

1. Show the engine working; never describe what could be demonstrated.
2. Every number is real output or a cited source.
3. Plain language over drafting vocabulary; name things as homeowners do.
4. Be explicit about what is free, what needs an account or Pro, what is unavailable during conversion, and what is a concept only.
5. The drawings are the hero; interface chrome stays out of their way.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Text on glass must meet contrast at the worst point of the backdrop. Full keyboard access to the studio and dialogs; respect reduced motion and reduced transparency. The project's axe scan (`scripts/a11y-scan.js`) must stay at zero violations.
